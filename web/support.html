Common questions

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send you a link to set a new password.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam folder. The email comes from Shor's sign-in service. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, email us from the address you signed up with.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the person's profile, the campaign, or your chat with them and tap Report or Block. In chat you can also press and hold a message to report it. Manage blocked accounts from Profile (or Account) → Blocked accounts.

Why can't I connect Instagram?

Instagram only shares insights for Creator or Business accounts. Switch for free in Instagram → Settings → Account type and tools. Your account needs at least 100 followers for audience demographics.

How does Shor's fee work?

When you raise an invoice, Shor's 10% fee is shown on it and deducted from the amount you receive.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile (or Account) → Delete account. See deleting your Shor account for other options and for what we keep.
