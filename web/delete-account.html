Delete your Shor account

This page explains how to delete your account in Shor (Shor – where creators meet brands), the app for Instagram creators and brands on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Shor and log in.
  2. Creators: open the Profile tab. Brands: open the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE, then tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted immediately. You can do the same on the Shor website after logging in.

Can't open the app?

Email chindhegaurav5@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Shor account". We confirm it's you, delete the account within 7 days, and reply when it's done.

What we delete

  • Your login, profile and settings.
  • Your campaigns or collaborations, drafts, submissions and chat messages.
  • Your Instagram connection, its access token, and all Instagram data and insights Shor stored.
  • Your private lists, blocks and the link between you and reports you sent.

What we keep

  • Invoices stay in the other party's records for accounting, with your name and contact details removed.
  • Reports about content that broke our rules are kept as moderation history.
  • Server logs held by our hosting providers are deleted on their standard schedule, usually within 90 days.

Only want to remove Instagram data? In the app, open Insights and tap Disconnect. Shor deletes all your Instagram data straight away and keeps your account.
