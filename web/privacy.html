1. Data we collect

Account details

  • Your email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider.
  • Whether you are a creator or a brand, your name and, for brands, the company name.
  • When you accepted our Terms of Use.

Profile details you choose to add

  • Creators: handle, bio, content niches, city and your usual rates.
  • Brands: website, industry and a description of the brand.

Content you create

  • Campaign briefs, applications and pitches, quoted prices, invitations and offers.
  • Links to drafts and to live Instagram posts, captions, notes and feedback.
  • Messages you send in Shor's chat, private lists of creators, people you block, and reports you send to us.

Business records

  • Agreed amounts, invoices, Shor's fee and invoice status.

Instagram data, only if a creator connects Instagram

Creators can choose to connect an Instagram professional (Creator or Business) account through Instagram's own login. You sign in on Instagram; we never see your Instagram password. With your permission, Shor reads:

  • Profile: username, name, profile photo, bio, website, account type, and follower, following and post counts.
  • Account insights for up to the last 90 days: reach, views, accounts engaged, interactions, likes, comments, shares, saves, replies and profile link taps.
  • Your 25 most recent posts and reels, plus any post you submit for a campaign: caption, type, date, link, thumbnail, likes and comments, and their insights (reach, views, saves, shares and interactions).
  • Follower demographics that Instagram reports in aggregate: age ranges, gender and top countries and cities. These describe groups of followers, not individual people.

Instagram's access token is stored encrypted and is only used by our servers.

Technical data

  • Our hosting providers record IP addresses and request logs to keep the service secure and working.
  • The app stores your login session on your device so you stay signed in.

Shor does not use advertising identifiers, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not include advertising or analytics tools.

2. How we use your data

  • To run Shor: accounts, campaigns, applications and invitations, content review, chat, reports and invoices.
  • To show brands a creator's verified public numbers (follower count and an engagement rate worked out from public like and comment counts), and to show creators their own insights dashboard.
  • To show a brand the insights of a post that a creator submitted for that brand's campaign.
  • To tell you when something needs you, such as an invitation, an approval, an invoice or a message, in the app and by email. You can turn the emails off in Help and settings.
  • To show the ratings and reviews brands and creators leave each other after a completed deal.
  • To publish your media kit, only if you turn it on.
  • To work out a creator quality score from public Instagram signals (engagement compared with similar-sized accounts, posting frequency, comments compared with likes, and sudden follower jumps), shown to brands to help them avoid fake followers.
  • To let brands find creators by audience, only for creators who turn on audience search.
  • To show brands which sales their creators drove: we count clicks on creators' tracked links, and, if a brand connects its Shopify or WooCommerce store, we receive its orders and keep only those made with a creator's discount code or tracked link (order number, amount, date and the code used). We do not store customers' names, addresses or contact details, and orders that match no creator are discarded.
  • To keep Shor safe: filtering offensive language, reviewing reports, enforcing blocks and our Terms of Use, and preventing fraud and abuse.
  • To respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run Shor. They process data only on our instructions and must protect it at least as well as this policy requires.

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, email for account messages, and server functions.
  • Vercel: hosting of the Shor website.
  • Resend: sending notification emails. It receives your email address, first name and the notification text.
  • Meta Platforms: Instagram's API, used only if you connect Instagram. Meta's own privacy policy applies to your use of Instagram.
  • Google Fonts: the app and website load fonts from Google, which receives your IP address when it serves them.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

Our providers may store and process data on servers outside India. Where they do, we rely on their contractual commitments to protect it.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and content: until you delete your account.
  • Instagram data: daily account insights for 90 days, daily follower counts for 365 days, and only your latest 25 posts plus posts submitted to campaigns. Disconnecting Instagram deletes all Instagram data and the access token immediately.
  • Invoices: kept for the other party's accounting records after you delete your account, but no longer linked to you.
  • Reports: kept as moderation history.
  • Server logs: kept by our providers for their standard security periods.

You also have the rights given by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and by other laws that apply to you, including the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Instagram access tokens are encrypted at rest. Database rules make sure each person can only reach the data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
